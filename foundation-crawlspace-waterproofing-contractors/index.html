Homeowner decisions

Different symptoms lead to different project decisions.

A homeowner noticing a foundation crack, a damp crawl space, or water entering a basement is not making the same decision. The first territory build focuses on one service and answers the questions that help that homeowner decide whether to request an inspection or estimate.

Foundation concern

Explain the visible concern, the limits of online information, what the contractor evaluates, and how the homeowner requests the next step.

Crawl-space concern

Explain moisture, odor, insulation, drainage, wood, structure, or comfort concerns only within the contractor’s actual service scope.

Basement-water concern

Explain where and when water appears, what the owner should document, and why a property-specific evaluation comes before a correction.

The website will not diagnose a structure, recommend a repair without evidence, invent local conditions, or promise a project outcome.

Before the review

Foundation and waterproofing contractor questions.

Useful details before you decide whether to book.

Is this only for foundation-repair companies?

No. It is for independent foundation-repair, crawl-space, and basement-waterproofing contractors in the United States. The first build still focuses on one primary service.

What does the 30-mile build include?

The $3,400 build covers one primary service and the final list of eligible towns you approve within an agreed 30-mile radius, up to 100. Every included town receives one main town-and-service page plus two original town-specific customer-question pages.

What does the initial build cost?

The one-time initial investment is $3,400: $1,700 to begin and $1,700 after staging approval, before launch.

Will this replace my current website?

No. The new customer-owned territory website runs alongside your current site.

Is the $500 monthly expansion required?

No. It is separate, optional, may be offered around month three only when enough usable search-performance data exists, and never starts automatically.

Does REFRDAI guarantee rankings, inquiries, or revenue?

No. REFRDAI does not guarantee indexing, rankings, inquiries, calls, estimates, projects, contracts, or revenue.

How does REFRDAI handle territory conflicts?

Before recommending a build, REFRDAI checks the proposed service, center, 30-mile radius, and final town list for overlap with active engagements. A review does not reserve a territory. Protection begins only after agreement acceptance and initial payment.

Do I have to pay each year to keep the website or domain?

No. You own and keep both. The optional $495 Territory Continuity Review includes the applicable domain renewal for that term and may renew territory protection after review and written renewal. If you decline it, protection expires and you pay the registrar directly for future domain renewal.

Can REFRDAI cover more than 30 miles?

Yes. Larger territories, inventories above 100 communities, and additional primary services require separate scope and pricing.

Is managed hosting included?

Yes, under normal usage. Unlimited hosting is not promised.
