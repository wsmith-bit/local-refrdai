Frequently asked questions

A few useful details before the review.

Is this only for foundation repair companies?

No. The pilot is for independent foundation repair, crawl-space, and basement-waterproofing contractors throughout the United States. Your company may offer one or several of these services.

What does "up to 100 communities" mean?

The $3,400 scope includes every suitable city or community on the approved 30-mile territory inventory for one primary service, up to a maximum of 100. Actual counts vary, and we do not add weak locations merely to reach the maximum. Every approved community receives the full three-page Digital Scout set.

How many pages does each community receive?

Every agreed included community receives one city and primary-service Digital Scout page plus two unique city-specific customer-question pages.

Can the initial build cover several services?

The company may offer several services, but the $3,400 initial build focuses on one agreed primary service.

Is the $500 monthly program required?

No. It is separate and optional, may be offered around month three only when sufficient usable search-performance data exists, and never starts automatically.

Is hosting included?

Managed hosting is included under normal usage. The offer does not promise free, unlimited, or lifetime hosting.

How long is the review?

The Territory Opportunity Review is a focused 30-minute Google Meet with William Smith.

Does booking commit me to purchase?

No. The review is free and creates no obligation. It is used to determine whether the build is a practical fit.

Can REFRDAI cover more than 30 miles?

Yes. Larger radiuses, inventories above 100 communities, and additional primary services require separate scope and pricing. We recommend 30 miles as the starting point for most contractors; companies serving a broader regional or multi-market territory can review a larger option with William.
